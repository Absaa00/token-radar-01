    How the value is worked out

    The model is a robust Huber regression (delta 1.345, fitted by IRLS) for log(market cap / TVL), fitted at run time on the 144 protocols in the snapshot that record a token and a market cap. Nine features, each centred on the live set’s mean: log TVL, 7-day TVL change (winsorised to ±15%), age in days, log chain count, and the interactions ch7d², ch7d·logTVL, ch7d·age and logTVL·age. The predicted multiple is exp(prediction), clamped to [0.02, 1.5]; implied value is TVL times that multiple. Nothing is a hardcoded list of names: rerun the program on the same snapshot and every coefficient below is refitted from the same figures.

    What a farm plan costs

    Every protocol on the board carries a six-step plan: fund a fresh wallet, deposit a sized amount, return weekly, work the points tasks, hold at least 30 days, and claim when a snapshot lands. Deposits are sized by implied value — $5,000 at $50M and up, $2,000 at $20M, $1,000 at $5M, $500 below that — and setup runs about $45 plus 60 minutes. The plan is a routine, not a promise: a launch may never come, and the implied value is a what-if, not a price.

    Reading the board honestly

    “Pre-token” here means the snapshot records no token for the protocol — DefiLlama’s record is not proof it has none. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out, as are versioned and exchange-named products, and a protocol at or above the $50M floor that the watchlist leaves out is a child product, not a launch of its own. The watchlist’s $50M floor is a floor, never a ceiling: the board also ranks 94 early protocols below it, down to $10M of TVL, because a launch worth farming can sit below the line.
