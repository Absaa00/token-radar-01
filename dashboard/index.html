How the value is fitted

log(mcap / TVL) = b0 + b1·log(TVL) + b2·change7d + b3·age + b4·log(chains)

Ordinary least squares on the snapshot’s live-token protocols. The 7-day change is winsorized to ±15% and the predicted multiple is clamped to [0.02, 1.5], so no single wild week and no outlier can put a protocol on top. Every coefficient is computed from the snapshot at run time.

    Methodology, in short

    Coverage: every protocol on the snapshot’s watchlist is ranked, plus pre-token protocols below the $50M floor with at least $10M TVL that pass the house filter (no bridges, no liquid staking or restaking, no CEX, no wrapped / staked / versioned names). Protocols above the floor enter only through the watchlist. Live-token protocols are not board rows; they are the comparables the model is fitted on, shown in the Live token tab.

    The model reads four facts per protocol — TVL, seven-day change, age since listing, chain count — and nothing else. No protocol name ever enters the fit. The fitted multiple is TVL’s own story: bigger protocols carry smaller multiples, fast weekly growth predicts a smaller multiple, older and multi-chain protocols carry slightly larger ones. The full validation is in 00-summary.md.
