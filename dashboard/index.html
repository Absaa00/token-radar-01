    How the ranking works

    Implied value = TVL × fitted multiple. The multiple comes from a Huber regression (delta 1.345) of log(market cap / TVL) on log TVL, 7-day TVL change (winsorized to ±15%), age, log chain count, and three interactions — fitted on the 128 live-token protocols in this snapshot, then clamped to [0.02, 1.5]. Huber down-weights the handful of live tokens trading at 20–400× TVL, so they cannot drag the fit. Leave-one-out validation (each live protocol predicted by a model fitted without it): median |log error| against for the median-ratio heuristic.
